Construction of a new 28 ° C water cooling system of Yulon II to satisfy Econyl and polymerization demands.


TARGERT USER:

Julon d.d., Aquafil S.p.A.

DESCRIPTION:

Due to lack of cooling power of 28 ° C (tower) water circuit and problems related to cooling towers operation during the hot summer period; it was decided to build a new cooling system on the roof of Yulon II. New installation of Econyl production raised cooling demand for 1 MW and will rise also in future. On the other hand the existing cooling system is located in the near vicinity of energy facility department, which is unfavorable mounting position, and the fact that towers are densely mounted they inevitably experience reduced cooling capacity during a hot season. Consequently the existing cooling circuits (three cooling loops - the process, air compressors and chillers) cannot provide optimum cooling temperature of 28 ° C due to increased wet bulb in summer (wet air recirculation). Another weakness of the existing loop of process cooling water is long delivery path from energetic department towards process users and the fact that the circuit is open at the highest point, leading into higher pumping head (5 bar) and consequently energy inefficient operation. New cooling installation consists of a cooling tower of 2 MW capacity, located on the roof of the central building Yulon II, and pumping/distribution station installed on the top floor. Pumping station includes also water treatment and filtration unit. With initiation the system will supply cooling water only to newly installed ECONYL production. With years the system is designed to be expended by additional two cooling towers with total cooling capacity of 6 MW, which is sufficient to cover all the cooling needs of the polymerization and Econyl together. With the gradual power transition from old to the new cooling installation, the existing "old" cooling system could be suspended, leading into improved operating conditions of the remaining towers near the energy facility.

Installation of pyrolysis furnace


TARGERT USER:

Julon d.d., Aquafil S.p.A.

DESCRIPTION:

A pyrolysis furnace from the manufacturer LUCKERATH was purchased for cleaning polymer residues from the melt filter of the Econyl production, as well as for cleaning spinneret packs from the spinning production. The main advantage of a pyrolysis furnace is minimal emission of harmful substances into the air. In the pyrolysis process the waste polymer slowly decomposes in the presence of a minimal proportion of oxygen and at elevated temperature. The flue gases released in this process are then, before being released into the atmosphere, completely burned in an additional combustion chamber at a temperature of 800 °C. The delivered pyrolysis furnace had to be installed on the 3rd floor of the polymerization building. For this purpose it was necessary to relocate some pipes in the production and free up space at the installation site, run gas to the installation site, bring the furnace in through a dedicated opening in the outer wall, install it, and build the 25 m high chimney that was supplied with the furnace.

Central control of chiller compressors in 7 °C water production


TARGERT USER:

Julon d.d., Aquafil S.p.A.

DESCRIPTION:

This investment is a logical continuation of investment 1385 and of the energy study by Cover Engineering, carried out in 2008. The main goal was the automation and optimization of the operation of the chiller plant, leading to electricity savings in the production of 7 °C water. For the control of the chiller plant, the control procedures of the individual parts of the system (control logic) were worked out and described in detail, on the basis of which TRANE programmed all the necessary control algorithms for the automatic operation of the plant.

In the process, the series of five pumps on the primary side was abandoned and removed, while the pumps on the distribution (consumer) side were modified and inverters added to adjust the capacity to the demand.

Construction of a low-pressure compressor station and distribution network

TARGERT USER:

Julon d.d., Aquafil S.p.A.

DESCRIPTION:

The investment was made for more efficient use of energy sources such as compressed air. In the spinning plants, 7 bar air is used for interlacing the filament and is reduced to <4.5 or <1.8 bar before use at the blowing nozzle. Since such use of compressed air is energetically wasteful, the energy study by Cover Engineering showed that an investment in a new low-pressure compressor station and distribution network would pay for itself within two years. As part of the investment, a new 2.5 bar network was built with a main supply pipeline and a low-pressure compressor station. The heart of the compressor station is two inverter-driven centrifugal compressors from the manufacturer K-Turbo, which are renowned for their favorable specific electricity consumption. The compressor station has a capacity of about 100 m3/min and on average operates with a flow of 60 m3/min of air at a supply pressure of 2.25 bar. The draw on the 7 bar network was thus reduced by the same capacity.
