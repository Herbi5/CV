DESCRIPTION:

Due to lack of cooling power of 28 Â° C (tower) water circuit and problems related to cooling towers operation during the hot summer period; it was decided to build a new cooling system on the roof of Yulon II. New installation of Econyl production raised cooling demand for 1 MW and will rise also in future. On the other hand the existing cooling system is located in the near vicinity of energy facility department, which is unfavorable mounting position, and the fact that towers are densely mounted they inevitably experience reduced cooling capacity during a hot season. Consequently the existing cooling circuits (three cooling loops - the process, air compressors and chillers) cannot provide optimum cooling temperature of 28 Â° C due to increased wet bulb in summer (wet air recirculation). Another weakness of the existing loop of process cooling water is long delivery path from energetic department towards process users and the fact that the circuit is open at the highest point, leading into higher pumping head (5 bar) and consequently energy inefficient operation. New cooling installation consists of a cooling tower of 2 MW capacity, located on the roof of the central building Yulon II, and pumping/distribution station installed on the top floor. Pumping station includes also water treatment and filtration unit. With initiation the system will supply cooling water only to newly installed ECONYL production. With years the system is designed to be expended by additional two cooling towers with total cooling capacity of 6 MW, which is sufficient to cover all the cooling needs of the polymerization and Econyl together. With the gradual power transition from old to the new cooling installation, the existing "old" cooling system could be suspended, leading into improved operating conditions of the remaining towers near the energy facility.
