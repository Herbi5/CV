MS Access application for reviewing of the results obtained from literature databases COMPENDEX and INSPEC

TARGERT USER:

Center for Experimental Mechanics (CEM).

DESCRIPTION:

Application developed on MS-Access and Visual Basic (VBA) platform, enables quick importing and reviewing of the results (as text files) obtained from literature databases Compendex and INSPEC. After file import the data are being filtered out and written into local Access database file. User friendly interface enables quickly reviewing of the results in a tabular form. Further filtering and manual selection into literature sub-list type A, B and C according to CEM specifications could be performed. Developed lists could be printed as reports and exported into other programs for further processing.
